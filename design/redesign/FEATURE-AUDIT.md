# Feature audit: every action in Appendix A against the engine

Each of the 453 actions in BRANCH-DESIGN-INTENT.md Appendix A, checked against the engine at `redesign/window` (engine code as on trunk ecc91c16).

- **engine** (196): the engine does it; wire it to the route given.
- **window** (187): window-only; no engine needed.
- **soon** (18): the engine can't do it yet; drawn greyed out ("Coming soon").
- **proto** (10): prototype scaffolding; not built into the real window.
- **offscope** (42): the phone app or keepoak.com; not in this window.

`public/app/core/features.js` holds the verdicts as `ready` (engine or window) and `soon`. A control is clickable only once its builder has wired it (`markLive`); `ready` alone never makes it clickable, so nothing looks wired that isn't.
The "why" column says when a control is only partly backed (a variant the engine refuses, a switch that must be on, wording the engine can't honour). Wire only what the engine does.
